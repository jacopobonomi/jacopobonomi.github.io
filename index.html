Stage 4 · Lore ~/about.md

About me

I love learning — and I'm fully aware that the more I know, the more I realize I don't. (I know that I know nothing)
Computer science has been my biggest obsession since day one. I started as a Linux sysadmin (GNU/Linux since 2007, open source forever), configuring servers and networks until I fell into security — CTFs, web vulnerabilities, the usual rabbit hole. I even have a Hackthebox profile if you want to add me.

From there I moved into web development at a corporate environment — legacy stack, Java, .NET, Angular — where I worked with seriously experienced people and learned a lot about software design.
After that I joined a startup, became CTO in under a year, got some shares, and learned a completely different set of lessons about money, people, and how companies actually work. We shipped 20+ projects across apps, SaaS, e-commerce, automations — basically everything.

Then I joined a smart consulting company, worked as a software engineer and team leader, had a lot of fun, and shipped a lot of backend across enterprise-scale projects for some of the largest companies in Europe.

Since the beginning of 2023 I'm Tech Lead at Giunti Editore, one of Italy's largest publishing groups. I manage a team of engineers and split my time between security, infrastructure and R&D: we are replacing legacy software, one piece at a time, with platforms we build in-house and that unify data across the whole group. We mostly write Go, Python and Next.js, on top of a single shared UI framework. Book publishing is a surprisingly interesting world.

During all these years I have done a lot of consultancy, to the most diverse companies, with many languages and software architectures.
I enjoy writing software, I do it for 80% of my time. The remaining 20% I like to use it to talk to interesting people, click here if you are one of them.
